CrossMatch: Math Puzzle Game — Privacy Policy

Developer: CrossMatch Games
Effective from first publication of this policy
Privacy contact: gamecrossmatch@gmail.com

Playing CrossMatch

This policy applies to the Google Play version of CrossMatch (com.crossmatch.game). You can play without creating an account. The app saves your game progress, active puzzles, rewards, restoration progress, settings, and optional local profile name and avatar on your device. The app uses this information to remember your progress and preferences.

The Google Play version does not transmit this gameplay or profile information to CrossMatch Games. It does not include advertising or analytics services.

Android backup and transfer

Depending on your device settings, Android can include selected app data in a system backup or copy it to another device. The app does not operate a separate cloud save service. Your device and backup settings control these system features.

Support and external links

If you choose to email us, your email application sends the message you choose to share. We use the reply address and information you provide to handle your request. Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary personal information. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Opening the policy link may use your browser; the host can process ordinary connection information to serve the page.

Managing your information

You can clear CrossMatch's local storage using your device settings. This removes local progress and settings from the installation; it does not delete a separate system backup. Manage backups through your device or backup provider. For questions or deletion requests about messages you sent to us, contact the email address listed above.

Changes

If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
